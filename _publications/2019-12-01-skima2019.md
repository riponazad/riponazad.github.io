This paper presents a scalable classification methodology based on decision tree induction applied to big data scenarios. The work explores ensemble and partitioning strategies to handle large volumes of data efficiently while maintaining classification accuracy.

**Authors:** S. Sabah, S. Z. B. Anwar, S. Afroze, **M. A. Azad**, S. Shatabda, D. M. Farid
