This work presents a low-complexity alternative to existing deep learning-based myocardial point tracking methods in 2D echocardiography. By redesigning the tracking pipeline for efficiency, the approach achieves competitive accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods while drastically reducing computational requirements — making it more feasible for deployment in clinical settings.
